About Dynamic Research Centre

Based in Kolkata, Dynamic Research Centre is a dedicated analytical testing and research facility committed to delivering precise, reliable, and timely scientific services. We support industries, municipalities, and institutions with rigorous testing across water, food, environmental, and material domains.

  • Strategic Location

    217 Chakdah, Kolkata 700093 — situated in the heart of South 24 Parganas, providing easy access for clients across West Bengal and beyond.

  • Experienced Team

    Our scientists and technicians bring decades of combined expertise in analytical chemistry, microbiology, and environmental monitoring.

  • Client‑Centric Approach

    We listen, customise, and deliver — every project is treated with the attention it deserves, from sample collection to final reporting.

  • Commitment to Ethical Practices

    Integrity and transparency are at the core of our operations. We adhere to strict quality protocols and maintain complete data confidentiality.

Testing & Analytical Services

Dynamic Research Centre offers a broad spectrum of testing services across multiple industries. Our laboratory is equipped to handle complex analytical challenges using recognised methodologies and state‑of‑the‑art instrumentation. All tests are performed with strict quality control and data integrity.

💧 Water Quality Testing

💧

Drinking Water Testing

Comprehensive analysis of drinking water for chemical, radiological, and microbiological parameters.

View parameters
  • pH, TDS, Hardness, Alkalinity, Chlorides
  • Nitrates, Fluorides, Phenolic Compounds, Cyanide
  • Heavy Metals: Arsenic, Mercury, Lead, Cadmium, Chromium
  • Microbiological: Total Coliforms, Pathogenic Organisms
  • Reference: BIS IS 10500, USEPA, APHA
🏭

Effluent Water Analysis

Testing of sewage and industrial effluent water to preserve water bodies and public health.

View parameters
  • BOD, COD, TOC, Chlorides, Ammoniacal Nitrogen
  • TKN, Residual Sodium Carbonate, Sulfates, Nitrates
  • Total Residual Chlorine, Fluorides, Sulfide
  • Oil and Grease, TSS, TDS
  • Reference: ISO 5667-10, EPA 1986
🌊

Surface Water Testing

Examination of streams, rivers, lakes, and wetlands to protect ecosystems and biodiversity.

View parameters
  • DO, Total Hardness, Chlorides, Calcium, Magnesium
  • Sodium Absorption Ratio, Percent Sodium, Free CO₂
  • Sulfates, Nitrates, Fluorides, Cyanide, Phenols
  • Anionic detergents, TDS, Oil and Grease
  • Reference: IS 2296
⚙️

Feed Water & Boiler Water Testing

Pre-treatment and in-process testing to minimize corrosion, deposition, and carryover in boiler systems.

View parameters
  • Feed Water: Total Hardness, pH, Conductivity, TDS
  • Boiler Water: Hardness, pH, TDS, Phosphate, Chloride
  • Oxygen, Silica, Iron + Copper, Oil
  • Instruments: UV-VS, FT-IR, pH Meter, GC, ICP-MS
  • Reference: IS 10496
🏊

Swimming Pool Water Testing

Monitoring of chemical balance and algal growth to ensure public health and safety.

View parameters
  • Total Alkalinity, Chloride, pH
  • Total Residual Chlorine, TDS
  • Iron, Colour, Turbidity
  • Heavy Metals (Lead)
  • Reference: IS 3328
🌾

Irrigation Water Quality Monitoring

Assessment of water quality for crop health, focusing on soluble salts, alkalinity, pH, and hardness.

View parameters
  • Soluble Salts, Alkalinity, pH, Hardness
  • Chloride, Sodium, Ion Toxicity
  • Components influencing plant productivity
  • Reference: IS 11624
⛰️

Ground Water Testing

Evaluation of chemical and biological constituents to ensure ground water quality for domestic, agricultural, and industrial use.

View parameters
  • pH, Turbidity, Colour, Odour
  • BOD, COD, TDS, Electrical Conductivity, Salinity
  • Chloride, Sulphide, Selenium, Magnesium, Potassium
  • Iron, Calcium, Lead, Chromium, Mercury
  • Reference: IS 10500

🌬️ Air Quality Monitoring

🌬️

Ambient Air Monitoring

Systematic, long-term assessment of outdoor pollutant levels to assess pollution extent and ensure compliance.

View parameters
  • All 12 mandatory parameters as per CPCB
  • PM2.5, PM10, SO₂, NOx, CO, O₃
  • Lead, Ammonia, Benzene, Benzo(a)pyrene
  • Data integration via LIMS for confidentiality
  • Reference Methods: BIS, USEPA, IS 10500
🏠

Indoor Air Quality Monitoring

Identification and quantification of contaminants in indoor environments to safeguard human health.

View parameters
  • VOC, Semi-VOC, Toxic Gases, Toxic Metals
  • Biological Pathogens
  • Temperature, Humidity, Lighting, Noise
  • Instruments: Hygrothermometer, Multi-Gas Analyser
  • Reference: ASHRAE, NIOSH, OSHA
🏗️

Work Zone Air Monitoring

Periodic monitoring of work environments to ensure safety from toxic dust/fumes at production sites.

View parameters
  • Sulphur Dioxide, Oxides of Nitrogen, Formaldehyde
  • Carbon Monoxide, Carbon Dioxide, VOC
  • Total Dust, Metals, PCB, PAH
  • PM10, PM2.5
  • Instruments: Handy Sampler, VOC sampler, AAS-GTA/VGA
🏭

Stack Emission Monitoring

Identification and quantification of pollutants from stacks to evaluate compliance and control efficiency.

View parameters
  • Criteria Pollutants, Hazardous Air Pollutants
  • Volatile Organic Compounds, Heavy Metals
  • Dioxins & Furans
  • Sample collection by trained techno-scientific personnel
  • Reference: Air (Prevention and Control of Pollution) Act 1981
💨

Air or Compressed Air Testing

Quality assessment of compressed air used in power tools, breathing apparatus, and industrial processes.

View parameters
  • Particle Count, Particle Concentration
  • Dew Point, Moisture, Total Oil
  • Microbial Count
  • Instruments: Particle Size Counter, Total Moisture Analyzer
  • Reference: ISO 8753

🧪 Food & Nutrition Testing

🧪

Nutritional Labelling

Accurate display of ingredients, calorific value, preservatives, shelf life, and legal requirements as per FSSAI.

View parameters
  • Ash, Fat, Crude Fiber, Moisture, Protein
  • Carbohydrate, Energy, Sugar, Salt, Dietary Fiber
  • Fatty Acid Profile, Cholesterol, Vitamins (A, B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B7, B9, B12, C, D, E, K)
  • Amino Acid, Organic Acid, Nucleotides
  • Instruments: AAS, ICP-OES, ICP-MS, HPLC-PDA/RID, GC-FID
