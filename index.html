Our Service Offerings

Dynamic Research Centre offers a comprehensive range of analytical testing services across water, air, soil, and food domains, using standardised methods and state‑of‑the‑art instrumentation.

Water Quality Analysis

Drinking Water Testing

Comprehensive analysis of drinking water for chemical, physical, and microbiological parameters.

Reference: IS 10500, IS 3025, IS 1622, APHA (24th Edition)

Wastewater Analysis

Testing of physico-chemical and microbiological parameters of raw and treated wastewater in relation with STP and ETP water to support public health and preservation of healthy aquatic environment.

Reference: IS 3025, IS 1622, APHA (24th Edition)

Surface Water Testing

Examination of streams, rivers, lakes, and wetlands to protect ecosystems and biodiversity.

Reference: IS 3025, IS 1622, APHA (24th Edition)

Irrigation Water Quality Analysis

Assessment of water quality for crop health.

Reference: IS 3025, APHA (24th Edition)

Ground Water Testing

Evaluation of physical, chemical and microbiological constituents to ensure ground water quality for domestic, agricultural, and industrial use.

Reference: IS 10500, IS 3025, IS 1622, APHA (24th Edition)

Air Quality Monitoring

Ambient Air Monitoring

Systematic assessment of outdoor pollutant levels to assess ambient air pollution extent.

Reference: IS 5182

Fugitive (industrial/process work zone) Emission Monitoring

Periodic monitoring of work environments to ensure safety from toxic dust/fumes at production/process sites.

Reference: IS 5182

Stack Emission Monitoring

Identification and quantification of pollutants from stacks to evaluate compliance and control efficiency.

Reference: IS 11255

Soil Testing

Comprehensive Soil Analysis

Comprehensive chemical analysis of soil samples for agricultural, environmental and industrial application.

Reference: IS 2720, ICAR soil testing manuals, IS 3025, APHA (24th Edition)

Food and Nutrition Testing

Food Chemical Testing

Evaluation of composition, quality, safety, authenticity and nutritional integrity of food products.

Reference: FSSAI lab manuals

Nutritional Labelling

Accurate display of nutritional ingredients, calorific value, and legal requirements as per FSSAI.

Reference: FSSAI lab manuals

Food Adulteration Testing

Detection of addition of inferior, harmful, or non-permitted substances in food products.

Reference: FSSAI lab manuals

Microbiological Analysis

Comprehensive assessment of safety, hygiene, shelf life, and microbial quality of food products.

Reference: FSSAI lab manuals for food products
